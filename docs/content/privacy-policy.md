Franklin — Privacy Policy
Effective Date:
2025-11-24
This Privacy Policy explains how DFrank Studioz (“we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects information in connection with Franklin BE (“Franklin”), our local-first AI middleware product. Your privacy is extremely important to us, and Franklin is intentionally designed to operate locally on your own machine with minimal data collection.
If you have questions, contact us at
support@dfrankstudioz.com
.
1. Overview
Franklin is a local-first AI system. This means:
Your files stay on your machine.
Your prompts stay local unless you explicitly enable cloud features.
We do not access, view, store, or transmit your personal data.
This Privacy Policy outlines the limited circumstances where information may be collected (e.g., purchasing a license, optional cloud fallback features, or customer support interactions).
2. What Franklin Does Not Collect
Franklin does not:
Upload your files to our servers
Log or store your prompts on our servers
Track or monitor your usage
Access your local file system remotely
Transmit personal data unless you explicitly choose cloud-based features
Everything you do inside Franklin stays on your machine unless you enable external APIs.
3. Information We Collect
We only collect information in these scenarios:
3.1 License Purchases
When you purchase Franklin BE through Stripe, we receive:
Your email address
License key activation status
Purchase details (amount, date, transaction ID)
We do NOT receive or store your credit card number.
3.2 Support Inquiries
If you email us or create a ticket, we may collect:
Your name (optional)
Your email
Technical information you choose to share (screenshots, logs, etc.)
3.3 Optional Cloud Features
Franklin includes optional features that use third-party APIs, such as OpenAI (GPT-4o, other cloud models). These features use your own API keys stored locally. If enabled, your prompts are sent only to the services you configure, never to us.
3.4 Analytics for Payments
To prevent fraud or duplicate licensing, Stripe may provide metadata such as IP country, device metadata, and purchase verification signals. We do not directly track or store these ourselves beyond what's needed to deliver and manage your license.
4. How We Use Your Information
We use your information only for:
Delivering and managing your license
Sending essential service emails (receipt, license key, support replies)
Providing customer support
Verifying legitimate usage (anti-fraud)
Improving Franklin and our website offerings
We never sell your information.
5. How Franklin Handles Your Local Data
5.1 Local-Only Mode
When local-only mode is enabled:
All processing is offline
No prompts leave your machine
No model requests hit external APIs
No data is shared with us or any third party
5.2 File Operations
Franklin includes tools such as list_dir, read_file, and summarize_file. These run locally and never transmit data externally.
5.3 Plugins
Plugins run locally. You choose which plugins to install or enable. Plugins may interact with the internet only if you explicitly approve.
6. Third-Party Services
Franklin may integrate with third-party APIs only if you turn them on:
Stripe — handles payments and license delivery
OpenAI / other LLM APIs — optional, if you add your API key
Cloudflare / Zapier — optional license automation
Each third-party service has its own privacy policy. You are responsible for reviewing and managing those settings.
7. Cookies and Website Data
Our website may use basic analytics (page visits, traffic sources) and cookies to maintain session state. Stripe checkout scripts are used for secure payment processing. Franklin the app does not use cookies.
8. Data Retention
We retain only:
Purchase and license details
Emails and support requests you send us
Basic Stripe records required for tax and legal compliance
We do not retain prompts, logs from your machine, file contents, or any Franklin system data.
9. Security
We use industry-standard security measures for license key storage, email support systems, Stripe transactions, and server-side operations (where applicable). However, Franklin runs on your own device. You are responsible for securing your machine and protecting your API keys.
