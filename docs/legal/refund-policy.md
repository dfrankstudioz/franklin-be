Franklin — Refund Policy
Last Updated:
24 November 2025
Thank you for supporting Franklin BE — Builder’s Edition. We want you to feel confident when purchasing Franklin, so the following Refund Policy outlines when refunds may be issued and how requests are handled.
1. Digital Product Nature
Franklin BE is a digital software product delivered instantly after purchase. Once the product is delivered, all sales are generally final because:
You immediately receive the full software package
You gain access to license keys
You receive ongoing updates and support
Digital goods cannot be “returned”
However, we understand that situations may arise where a refund is appropriate.
2. Eligibility for Refunds
Refunds may be granted under the following conditions:
a. Accidental Duplicate Purchases
If you accidentally purchased Franklin more than once, we will refund the extra purchase(s).
b. Technical Issues Preventing Use
If you experience a technical issue that prevents you from using Franklin, and:
You have contacted support at
support@dfrankstudioz.com
We have attempted reasonable troubleshooting, and
The issue cannot be resolved within 14 days,
a refund may be issued.
c. Non-Delivery of Product
If, for technical reasons, your license key or download email was never delivered and we cannot confirm successful delivery, you may request a refund.
3. Non-Refundable Situations
Refunds will not be granted for the following:
Change of mind after receiving the product
Inability to run Franklin due to unsupported platforms or not meeting system requirements (e.g., attempting to run without Docker, on unsupported hardware, or on restricted hosting environments)
Failure to follow setup instructions
Claiming dissatisfaction without allowing Franklin Support to troubleshoot the issue
Purchases older than 30 days
4. Refund Window
Refund requests must be submitted within 30 days of purchase. After this period, all sales are final.
5. How to Request a Refund
Please email:
📧
support@dfrankstudioz.com
Include:
Full name
Email used for purchase
Stripe receipt ID
Reason for refund request
Any relevant screenshots or logs (optional)
We typically respond within 48 hours.
6. Processing of Refunds
Approved refunds are issued back to the original payment method. Processing may take 3–10 business days depending on your bank. Once refunded, your license key will be deactivated. Continued use of Franklin after refund is prohibited under the EULA.
7. Chargebacks
If a chargeback is filed without first contacting support:
Your license key may be immediately deactivated
You may lose access to updates and support
We reserve the right to dispute invalid chargebacks with Stripe
We always encourage reaching out to
support@dfrankstudioz.com
first — most issues can be resolved quickly.
8. Policy Updates
This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect improvements or legal requirements. The latest version is always available on the Franklin website.
